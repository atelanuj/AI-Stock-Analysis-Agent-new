# Stock AI Agent V8.2 Features

V8.2 is focused on making technical analysis easier to read while keeping V8/V8.1 functionality intact.

## New in V8.2

- Redesigned **Technical Summary** panel with bullish / neutral / bearish visual bars.
- Redesigned **Support & Resistance Map** with beginner-friendly R1/R2/R3, S1/S2/S3, PRICE and PIVOT display.
- Added a compact **indicator verdict table** beside support/resistance.
- Added target and stop-loss **color coding** in the AI Technical Decision card.
- Added **AI Recommended Stocks** in the Screener section.
- Added more **info icons** across sections with simple explanations and examples.
- Preserved existing Stocks, Funds, Screener, Intraday, IPO, Portfolio, Watchlist and chatbot features.

## Notes

- AI Recommended Stocks in the screener are heuristic shortlists built from scores, trend bias, momentum and price-structure context.
- The explanation modal is designed for beginner users and uses plain language.
